The history of twenty one pilots

Twenty One Pilots were formed in Columbus, Ohio, on December 29, 2009, by Tyler Joseph, Nick Thomas, and Chris Salih. The band independently released their self-titled debut album later that year and quickly began building a local following through live performances. In 2010, they uploaded two tracks to SoundCloud: a cover of"Jar of Hearts" by Christina Perri and the original song"Time to Say Goodbye", which is often considered one of the earliest songs associated with the band.

In 2011, founding members Chris Salih and Nick Thomas left the band. Soon after, Josh Dun joined Tyler Joseph, creating the two-member lineup that continues today. The duo released their second independent album, Regional at Best, later that year and gained a larger following in their hometown of Columbus. In 2012, Twenty One Pilots signed with Fueled by Ramen. The following year, they released Vessel, their first major-label album. Featuring songs such as"Holding on to You," "Car Radio," "House of Gold," and"Guns for Hands," the album introduced many listeners to the band's unique blend of alternative rock, rap, and electronic music. Vessel was a major success and helped establish Twenty One Pilots as a rising force in alternative music.
